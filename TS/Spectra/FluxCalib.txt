I have calibrated the fluxes for the rotated spectra. I only found B and V magnitudes (tycho-2 catalogue) for all of them, thus I only used those 2 magnitudes and the parallax distance. The factor alpha is the one by which you have to multiply the spectrum (10^y, where y is the second column, log F_lambda) to get the value at 1 AU. Files (rotated spectra) are in my strider home directory. The reason why the factor for beta Pic is larger is that the model spectrum (previous version) has a smaller magnitude.
